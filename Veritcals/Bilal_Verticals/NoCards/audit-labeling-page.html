Internal Audit Reports

Audit title and report number, audit objective, scope and period, methodology description, finding numbers, finding descriptions, risk ratings (critical, high, medium, low), root cause analysis, recommendations, management responses (agree, partially agree, disagree), responsible parties, remediation target dates, follow-up status (open, in progress, closed, overdue), and executive summary sections parsed across internal audit department report formats and co-sourced engagement deliverables.

📜

SOX Compliance Documentation

Control identification numbers, control objectives, control descriptions, control type (preventive, detective, corrective), control frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual), process owner names, key control designations, testing procedures, sample sizes, test results (effective, exception noted, deficiency), deficiency classifications (significant deficiency, material weakness), remediation plans, management certifications (Section 302), and auditor attestation opinions (Section 404) structured for SOX compliance tracking and reporting pipelines.

🔍

SOC 1 & SOC 2 Audit Reports

Service organization name, report type (Type I or Type II), report period, trust service criteria covered (security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy), system description boundaries, control objectives, control activities, complementary user entity controls (CUECs), complementary subservice organization controls (CSOCs), tests of controls and results, exceptions noted, service auditor opinion (unqualified, qualified, adverse, disclaimer), and management assertion details extracted across AICPA-standard SOC report formats.

⚠

Risk Assessment Matrices

Risk identification numbers, risk descriptions, risk categories (operational, financial, compliance, strategic, reputational, technology), likelihood ratings (rare, unlikely, possible, likely, almost certain), impact ratings (insignificant, minor, moderate, major, catastrophic), inherent risk scores, control effectiveness ratings, residual risk scores, risk appetite thresholds, risk owners, key risk indicators (KRIs), mitigation strategies, and escalation triggers labeled for enterprise risk management and risk monitoring dashboards.

✅

Corrective Action Plans (CAPs)

Finding reference numbers, original audit finding descriptions, root cause determinations, corrective action descriptions, action step details, responsible parties (names and titles), milestone dates, completion target dates, actual completion dates, evidence of completion requirements, validation testing procedures, status indicators (not started, in progress, completed, verified, overdue), escalation history, and management sign-off fields structured for remediation tracking and audit follow-up automation.

🏛

ISO & Regulatory Examination Reports

Certification body name, standard reference (ISO 9001, 27001, 14001, 22301, 45001), audit type (certification, surveillance, recertification), scope of certification, clause references, nonconformity classifications (major, minor, observation, opportunity for improvement), nonconformity descriptions, objective evidence cited, corrective action requirements, closure deadlines, certification decision (granted, continued, suspended, withdrawn), regulatory examination findings, matters requiring attention (MRAs), and consent order terms extracted across ISO registrar reports and regulatory examination letters.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is audit and compliance document labeling?
Audit and compliance document labeling annotates internal audit reports, SOX documentation, risk assessments, SOC 2 reports, and other governance documents with structured labels that ML models use as training data. Each region is tagged with its semantic role (finding numbers, risk ratings, control descriptions, framework references, remediation deadlines, responsible parties, testing results, management responses) and exported in a format your pipeline can consume directly.
Which audit and compliance document types are supported?
Internal audit reports, SOX compliance documentation (Section 302 and 404 reports), SOC 1 and SOC 2 audit reports (Type I and Type II), risk assessment matrices, compliance checklists, corrective action plans (CAPs), regulatory examination responses, ISO audit reports (9001, 27001, 14001), HIPAA compliance assessments, anti-money laundering (AML) reports, policy attestation records, control testing workpapers, management representation letters, and whistleblower investigation summaries.
How accurate is automated audit document labeling?
Labels are typically 91%+ accurate on standard audit document layouts. Internal audit reports, SOC 2 reports, and ISO audit documents follow structured frameworks with consistent formatting conventions, which allows the model to achieve strong baseline accuracy. Confidence scores attach to every label so low-confidence regions can be flagged for manual review before GRC automation or compliance monitoring runs.
What export formats are supported?
Structured JSON with bounding boxes, segment text, label classifications, and confidence scores. Markdown is also supported. Both are directly compatible with PyTorch DataLoaders, TensorFlow Datasets, and Hugging Face Transformers.
Is the tool free?
Yes. Label your first 5 pages without an account. Larger batches and API access are available on paid plans.
Is sensitive audit and compliance data secure?
Yes. Documents are processed over encrypted connections, never used for model training without explicit consent, and can be deleted immediately after labeling. Enterprise plans include SOC 2 compliance, private deployment options, and data handling protocols suitable for internal audit teams, external audit firms, compliance departments, GRC platform providers, and regulated organizations with strict confidentiality requirements for audit workpapers, control deficiency findings, and remediation plans.
Can this help with GRC automation and continuous compliance monitoring?
Yes. The labeling output structures finding numbers, risk ratings, control descriptions, framework references, remediation deadlines, responsible parties, testing results, evidence references, and management responses into machine-readable formats that feed directly into GRC platforms, continuous compliance monitoring dashboards, audit management systems, and regulatory reporting tools that automate finding tracking, risk scoring, control testing documentation, and remediation workflow management.
